Silt Settler Nomogram

Preliminary design of a straight compartmented silt settler for cohesive sediment at a candidate weir site

Inputs

Parameters (defaults from the paper)
Ct = 0.35 calibrated from 3-D simulations of a straight four-compartment basin; τd is the Krone deposition threshold; qmax is the upper bound of the 0.5–1.5 m/h range for fine sediment.

Results

(a) Settler length L against settling velocity Vs for target efficiencies; dash-dot line is the surface-loading floor Lq; marker = your design.
(b) Compartment width b (N = 4, 5, 6) and total width B (dashed, right axis) against discharge Q; marker = your design.

Design equations

τb = ρ g n² U² / h1/3(1)
Ud = (τd h1/3 / (ρ g n²))1/2(2)
B = Q / (Ud h),  b = B / N(3)
η = 1 − exp(−Ct Vs A / Q)(4)
Ls = Ud h ln(1/(1 − η*)) / (Ct Vs)(5)
Lq = Ud h / qmax(6)
L = max(Ls, Lq)(7)
ηL = 1 − exp(−Ct Vs L / (Ud h))(8)
Vs = (ρs − ρ) g d² / (18 μ)(9)
ρ = 1000 kg/m³, g = 9.81 m/s². The settler length is independent of discharge; discharge sets only the width. The sediment fixes the plan area A, so a site that cannot take the length should widen the settler (U below Ud) rather than shorten it. The procedure is for preliminary design at the weir-siting stage and must be verified by CFD and physical modelling before detailed design.
